[h1]Slurg the Slime[/h1]

A scavenger who turns the world's garbage into growth. Slurg starts small, frail
and slow, then eats his way into becoming the biggest thing on the map.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Eat garbage, grow[/h2]

Every piece of filth he swallows raises his [b]Level[/b]. The cap is Level 5000.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]What fullness changes[/h2]

Level only sets his [b]ceiling[/b]. How much of it he actually gets is decided by
how full he is. Size and speed slide together as his belly fills, from [b]1%[/b]
full up to [b]90%[/b] full, where both peak.

Speed is measured against his own nominal pace, size against his own empty size.

[table]
[tr][th]Belly[/th][th]Level 0 size[/th][th]Level 0 speed[/th][th]Max size[/th][th]Max speed[/th][/tr]
[tr][td]Empty[/td][td]x1.00[/td][td]x0.75[/td][td]x1.00[/td][td]x0.75[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]25%[/td][td]x1.13[/td][td]x0.88[/td][td]x1.54[/td][td]x1.09[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]50%[/td][td]x1.28[/td][td]x1.03[/td][td]x2.10[/td][td]x1.44[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]75%[/td][td]x1.42[/td][td]x1.17[/td][td]x2.66[/td][td]x1.79[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]90% or more[/td][td]x1.50[/td][td]x1.25[/td][td]x3.00[/td][td]x2.00[/td][/tr]
[/table]

An [b]empty belly is the same floor at every level[/b], a quarter below his
nominal pace, however much he has eaten in his life. Filling up is worth
[b]+67% speed[/b] to a fresh Slurg and [b]+167%[/b] to a capped one.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Regeneration[/h2]

He slowly heals himself, but only while fed. Rate measured against [i]its own[/i]
slowest setting.

[table]
[tr][th]Belly[/th][th]Rate[/th][th]Relative[/th][/tr]
[tr][td]Below 50%[/td][td]none[/td][td]off[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]50% full[/td][td]1 hp / 60s[/td][td]x1[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]70% full[/td][td]1 hp / 35s[/td][td]x1.7[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]90% full or more[/td][td]1 hp / 10s[/td][td]x6[/td][/tr]
[/table]

Levelling doubles the whole curve: a fresh Slurg regenerates at half rate, a
capped one at full rate.

Each garbage food restores a flat amount [b]plus a share of his stomach[/b], so
the hunger figures are shown as a range: the low number is Level 0, the high
number is the cap. Rot is 1 + 1% of stomach, Compost is 15 + 6%, and so on.

[b]Poop, Guano and Compost are food for Slurg alone.[/b] No other character can
eat them.

[b]Iron gut.[/b] Nothing in the game can drain his health, hunger or sanity.
Mushrooms are the one exception: their toxicity still bites, but it lands on his
mind rather than his body.
