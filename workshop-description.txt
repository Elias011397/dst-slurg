[h1]Slurg the Slime[/h1]

A scavenger who turns the world's garbage into growth. Slurg starts small, frail
and slow, then eats his way into becoming the biggest thing on the map.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Eat garbage, grow[/h2]

Eating any type of rotted food or excrement will raise his [b]Level[/b], up to a
cap of 5000.

[table]
[tr][th]Food[/th][th]Health[/th][th]Hunger[/th][th]Sanity[/th][th]Levels[/th][/tr]
[tr][td]Rot[/td][td]+3[/td][td]1 + 1%[/td][td]+1[/td][td]+1[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Spoiled Fish[/td][td]+3[/td][td]1 + 1%[/td][td]+1[/td][td]+1[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Poop[/td][td]+4[/td][td]10 + 3%[/td][td]+5[/td][td]+2[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Guano[/td][td]+5[/td][td]10 + 4%[/td][td]+5[/td][td]+2[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Wet Goop[/td][td]+5[/td][td]5 + 3%[/td][td]+5[/td][td]+3[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Compost[/td][td]+15[/td][td]15 + 6%[/td][td]+15[/td][td]+5[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Rotten Egg[/td][td]+25[/td][td]5 + 5%[/td][td]+10[/td][td]+10[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Glommer's Goop[/td][td]+50[/td][td]20 + 10%[/td][td]+50[/td][td]+25[/td][/tr]
[/table]

[b]The % is a share of his stomach[/b], so the same garbage feeds him more as he
grows. Rot is 1 + 1%: worth [b]2 hunger[/b] on a 100 stomach, and [b]11[/b] on a
1000 one.

Normally every one of these is either harmful or inedible. [b]Poop, Guano and
Compost are food for Slurg alone[/b] and no other character can eat them.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]What levelling raises[/h2]

[table]
[tr][th][/th][th]Level 0[/th][th]Level 5000[/th][/tr]
[tr][td]Health[/td][td]50[/td][td]300[/td][/tr]

Health, stomach and damage are simply his. Speed and size are [b]caps[/b] he only
reaches on a full stomach. Levelling raises the ceiling; eating decides how close
he gets to it.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]How full he is decides the rest[/h2]

He swells and speeds up as his belly fills, from empty up to [b]90% full[/b],
where both peak at the caps above.

[table]
[tr][th]Belly[/th][th]Speed, Level 0[/th][th]Speed, Level 5000[/th][th]Size, Level 0[/th][th]Size, Level 5000[/th][/tr]
[tr][td]Empty[/td][td]x0.75[/td][td]x0.75[/td][td]x1.00[/td][td]x1.00[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Half[/td][td]x1.03[/td][td]x1.44[/td][td]x1.28[/td][td]x2.10[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]90% or more[/td][td]x1.25[/td][td]x2.00[/td][td]x1.50[/td][td]x3.00[/td][/tr]
[/table]

An empty belly drops him to the [b]same floor at every level[/b], a quarter below
his usual pace, however much he has eaten in his life.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Slime stomach[/h2]

[b]Nothing in the game can drain his health, hunger or sanity.[/b] Spoiled food
loses none of its value to him, and monster meat costs him nothing.

[table]
[tr][th]Category[/th][th]Normally[/th][th]For Slurg[/th][/tr]
[tr][td]Raw meat[/td][td]full value[/td][td]half value[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Monster food[/td][td]full value[/td][td]half value[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Gears[/td][td]+60 / 75 / +50[/td][td]+10 / 12.5 / +10[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Mushrooms[/td][td]costs health[/td][td]costs sanity instead[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Everything else[/td][td]penalties apply[/td][td]no penalties at all[/td][/tr]
[/table]

Mushrooms are the one exception. Their toxicity still bites, but it lands on
his mind rather than his body.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Slime regeneration[/h2]

He heals himself, but only while fed. Below half a stomach it stops entirely.

[table]
[tr][th]Belly[/th][th]Healing[/th][/tr]
[tr][td]Under half[/td][td]none[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Half[/td][td]1 hp every 60s[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]90% or more[/td][td]1 hp every 10s[/td][/tr]
[/table]

A fresh Slurg heals at half these rates, a capped one at full.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]The catch[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Fragile start.[/b] 50 health and a 100 stomach.
[*][b]Always hungry.[/b] He burns food 35% faster than anyone else.
[*][b]Feast or famine.[/b] Empty means small, slow, and no healing.
[/list]
