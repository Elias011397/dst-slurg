[h1]Slurg the Slime[/h1]

A scavenger who turns the world's garbage into growth. Slurg starts small, frail
and slow, then eats his way into becoming the biggest thing on the map.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]How he eats, in short[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Food can never cost him health or hunger.[/b]
[*][b]Harm becomes madness.[/b] Health damage is added onto the food's sanity cost, so anything that would have hurt him drains his mind instead. [i]Mushrooms still get him this way.[/i]
[*][b]Rot is a meal.[/b] Spoiled food loses none of its value to him.
[*][b]Monster food and raw meat are free.[/b] Neither carries a penalty.
[*][b]Filth is food.[/b] Poop, Guano and Compost are edible for Slurg alone.
[*][b]Fungus fills him.[/b] Every mushroom restores an extra 2% of his stomach.
[*][b]Garbage makes him grow.[/b] Rotted food and excrement raise his Level.
[/list]

Read as [i]health / hunger / sanity[/i]. Durian shows the rule: its own -5 sanity
plus the -3 that would have hit his health comes to [b]-8[/b]. A food that
restored sanity restores less instead, which is why the Cooked Blue Cap gives +7
rather than +10. Ordinary food like the Carrot is untouched.

[b]Mushrooms are the one thing that still really hurts him.[/b] A raw Red Cap
normally takes 20 health and no sanity at all, so for Slurg it takes [b]20
sanity[/b] and nothing else. A raw Green Cap keeps its full -50. He is immune to
almost everything, and then walks into a mushroom.

They do feed him, though. Every mushroom gives [b]2% of his stomach[/b] on top
of whatever hunger it already had. That is only 2 points early on, but 20 once
his stomach reaches 1000, which is enough to make a cooked cap worth eating when
it feeds nobody else at all. Fungus is food and poison at once.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Eat garbage, grow[/h2]

Eating any type of rotted food or excrement will raise his [b]Level[/b], up to a
cap of 5000.

Health, stomach and damage are simply his. Speed and size are [b]caps[/b] he only
reaches on a full stomach. Levelling raises the ceiling; eating decides how close
he gets to it.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]How full he is decides the rest[/h2]

He swells and speeds up as his belly fills, from empty up to [b]90% full[/b],
where both peak at the caps above.

his usual pace, however much he has eaten in his life.

[h2]Slime regeneration[/h2]

He heals himself, but only while fed. Below half a stomach it stops entirely.
Above that it speeds up the fuller he is, and again the further he has levelled.

[h2]The catch[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Fragile start.[/b] 50 health and a 100 stomach.
[*][b]Always hungry.[/b] He burns food 35% faster than anyone else.
[*][b]Feast or famine.[/b] Empty means small, slow, and no healing.
[*][b]It goes to his head.[/b] Harmful food spares his body and takes his mind.
[/list]
