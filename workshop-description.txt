[h1]Slurg the Slime[/h1]

Slurg eats rot, droppings and anything else that has gone bad. He starts weak
and slow, and every piece of garbage he eats makes him permanently bigger,
tougher and faster.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]How he eats[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Nothing can cost him health or hunger.[/b] Damage that would have hurt him is added onto the food's sanity cost instead.
[*][b]He eats what nobody else can.[/b] Poop, Guano and Compost are food for Slurg alone. Rot keeps its full value, and monster meat and raw meat carry no penalty at all.
[*][b]Mushrooms are the exception.[/b] Their health damage becomes sanity damage, so a raw Red Cap costs him 20 sanity. They also give 2% of his stomach on top of their own hunger.
[*][b]Garbage makes him grow.[/b] Rotted food and excrement raise his Level, up to a cap of 5000.
[/list]

Read as [i]health / hunger / sanity[/i]. Durian: its own -5 sanity plus the -3
health becomes [b]-8 sanity[/b]. The shift works both ways, so a Cooked Blue Cap
gives +7 instead of +10. Food with no health penalty, like the Carrot, is
unchanged.

The first four are simply his. Speed and size are only [b]caps[/b]: levelling
raises the ceiling, and how full he is decides how close he gets to it. He swells
and speeds up as his belly fills, peaking at [b]90% full[/b].

An empty belly gives the [b]same result at every level[/b]: normal size, and a
quarter below his usual speed.

[h2]Slime regeneration[/h2]

He heals himself, but only while fed. Below half a stomach it stops entirely.
Above that it runs faster the fuller he is, and faster again the further he has
levelled. Seconds per hit point:

[hr][/hr]
[h2]The catch[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]A slow start.[/b] 50 health, a 100 stomach, and rot worth only 2 hunger each.
[*][b]Always hungry.[/b] He burns food 35% faster than anyone else.
[*][b]Everything depends on his belly.[/b] Empty means slow, small and no healing, whatever his level.
[/list]
