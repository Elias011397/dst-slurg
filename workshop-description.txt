[h1]Slurg the Slime[/h1]

A scavenger who turns the world's garbage into growth. Slurg starts small, frail
and slow, then eats his way into becoming the biggest thing on the map.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Eat garbage, grow[/h2]

Eating any type of rotted food or excrement will raise his [b]Level[/b], up to a
cap of 5000.

[b]Poop, Guano and Compost are food for Slurg alone[/b] and no other character
can eat them.

he gets to it.

He swells and speeds up as his belly fills, from empty up to [b]90% full[/b],
where both peak at the caps above.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Slime stomach[/h2]

[b]Food cannot drain his health, hunger or sanity[/b], with one exception:
anything that would normally hurt him takes that damage out of his [b]sanity[/b]
instead. It is the only way eating can cost him anything at all.

[tr][td]Monster Meat[/td][td]-20 / 18.75 / -15[/td][td]0 / 9.375 / 0[/td][/tr]
[/table]

Only the health damage carries across. A food's own sanity drain is wiped like
everything else, so the sanity he loses is always exactly the harm that would
have hit his body. Monster food is the exception twice over: his stomach throws
out its penalties before the shift can reach them, so it costs him nothing.

Raw meat, monster food and gears are all halved. He wants garbage, not groceries.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Slime regeneration[/h2]

He heals himself, but only while fed. Below half a stomach it stops entirely.

[table]
[tr][th]Belly[/th][th]Healing[/th][/tr]
[tr][td]Under half[/td][td]none[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Half[/td][td]1 hp every 60s[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]90% or more[/td][td]1 hp every 10s[/td][/tr]
[/table]

A fresh Slurg heals at half these rates, a capped one at full.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]The catch[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Fragile start.[/b] 50 health and a 100 stomach.
[*][b]Always hungry.[/b] He burns food 35% faster than anyone else.
[*][b]Feast or famine.[/b] Empty means small, slow, and no healing.
[*][b]It goes to his head.[/b] Harmful food spares his body and takes his mind.
[/list]
