[h1]Slurg the Slime[/h1]

Slurg eats rot, droppings and anything else that has gone bad. He starts frail and
no quicker than anyone else, and every piece of garbage he eats makes him
permanently bigger, tougher and faster.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]How he eats[/h2]

[b]Nothing can cost him health or hunger.[/b] Damage that would have hurt him is
added onto the food's sanity cost instead.

[b]He eats what nobody else can.[/b] Poop, Guano and Compost are food for Slurg
alone. Rot keeps its full value, and monster meat and raw meat carry no penalty.

[b]Mushrooms are the exception.[/b] Their health damage becomes sanity damage,
and they give 2% of his stomach on top of their own hunger.

[b]Garbage makes him grow.[/b] Rotted food and excrement raise his Level, to a
cap of 5000.

His belly gives him a speed bonus either side of normal: a quarter slower when
empty, dead level with everyone at half full, a quarter faster at three quarters.
Levelling then pulls the two halves apart. The reward for being full grows to
three times its size, while the penalty for being empty shrinks away to nothing,
so a maxed Slurg runs from x1.00 to x1.75 with no downside left at all.
Multipliers are against Wilson's run speed, so x1.00 means exactly as fast as
Wilson.

Size is x1.50 at level 0 and x3.00 at level 5000, whatever he has eaten. It is
cosmetic, and does not change how fast he moves.

For scale: a full level 0 Slurg matches a Walking Cane (x1.25), and a full maxed
Slurg just edges out a Hound (x1.67). A starving one is a quarter slower than
everyone to begin with, and exactly average by the time he is maxed.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]The catch[/h2]

[b]A fragile start.[/b] 50 health, a 100 stomach, and rot worth only 2 hunger
each.

[b]Always hungry.[/b] He burns food 35% faster than anyone else.

[b]An empty belly stops the healing dead.[/b] Below half full he regenerates
nothing at all, at any level. The speed penalty for it does at least wear off as
he grows, but the healing never comes back until he eats.
