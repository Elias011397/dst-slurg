[h1]Slurg the Slime[/h1]

A scavenger who turns the world's garbage into growth. Slurg starts small, frail
and slow, then eats his way into becoming the biggest thing on the map.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]How he eats[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Nothing can cost him health or hunger.[/b] Damage that would have hurt him is added onto the food's sanity cost instead, so harm goes to his mind and never his body.
[*][b]He eats what nobody else can.[/b] Poop, Guano and Compost are food for Slurg alone. Rot keeps its full value, and monster meat and raw meat carry no penalty at all.
[*][b]Mushrooms are the exception.[/b] They cannot reach his body, so they go for his mind instead, which makes a raw Red Cap the worst thing he can swallow. They also feed him 2% of his stomach on top of their own hunger. Fungus is food and poison at once.
[*][b]Garbage makes him grow.[/b] Rotted food and excrement raise his Level, up to a cap of 5000.
[/list]

Read as [i]health / hunger / sanity[/i]. Durian is the clearest case: its own -5
sanity plus the -3 that would have hit his health comes to [b]-8[/b]. The shift
works both ways, so a food that restored sanity restores less, which is why the
Cooked Blue Cap gives +7 rather than +10. Ordinary food like the Carrot is
untouched.

sized and a quarter below his usual pace, however much he has eaten in his life.

[h2]Slime regeneration[/h2]

He heals himself, but only while fed. Below half a stomach it stops entirely.
Above that it runs faster the fuller he is, and faster again the further he has
levelled. Seconds per hit point:

[hr][/hr]
[h2]The catch[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]A slow start.[/b] 50 health, a 100 stomach, and rot worth 2 hunger a piece. The first levels are a grind.
[*][b]Always hungry.[/b] He burns food 35% faster than anyone else.
[*][b]It all rides on his belly.[/b] Let it empty and he is slow, small, and no longer healing, however far he has come.
[/list]
